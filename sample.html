01 / Who we are

Technology is for everyone. Understanding starts with language.

AlgoFilipino is a nonprofit initiative that aims to enrich the way Filipinos learn programming and other technologies by making technical concepts more understandable through the Filipino language.

We envision a learning environment where students, young people, and technology enthusiasts can explore digital skills without feeling that English proficiency is a prerequisite to understanding how technology works.

Our advocacy is simple: make knowledge more accessible, encourage curiosity, and help more Filipinos participate in the digital world. Technology has its own language, but learning it can begin in ours.

"Hindi dapat maging hadlang ang wika sa pagkatuto." OUR GUIDING PRINCIPLE

06 / Frequently asked questions

Getting to know AlgoFilipino.

A quick introduction to our advocacy and the ideas behind it.

AlgoFilipino is a nonprofit initiative focused on making programming and technology more understandable through the Filipino language.
Learning in a familiar language can help people engage with new ideas. Our goal is to make technical concepts more approachable while recognizing that programming languages and technical terms may still use English.
Students, young people, beginners, educators, and anyone interested in understanding technology can benefit from accessible explanations and shared learning.
